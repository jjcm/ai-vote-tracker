Every bill on this site is put to five language models. Each one reads the bill title, the summary, and an excerpt of the text, then returns a single binding answer — Yes or No on final passage — with one sentence explaining the decisive consideration. There is no abstain option, because a tally of maybes tells you nothing.

Verdicts are cached, so a model votes on a given bill once and that answer stands until the bill is re-run. The alignment page multiplies those votes by an ideology score assigned to each bill and averages the result into a single position between −1.0 and +1.0.

A caveat worth reading

These are model outputs, not endorsements, and not the positions of the organizations that build the models. A different prompt, a different day, or a newer checkpoint can move a verdict. Read the site as a record of what these systems said when asked, and nothing more.
